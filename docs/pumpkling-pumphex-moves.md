# Pumpkling and Pumphex references

Moveset and ultimate: https://i.e-z.host/📸/bghsukkf.gif
Legendary trait: https://i.e-z.host/📸/18vb08bp.png

Regular moves in recorded order: Tackle, Torment, Leaf Blade, Smoke Cover, Shadow Ambush, Leech Seed, Nightfall Slash, Rally, Blossom Strike, Leaf Razor, Immolate, Shadow Convergence. Learning levels use the established dex progression (1, 1, 10, 20, 40, 50, 60, 70, 90, 100, 120, 140), rather than individually observed unlock levels. Existing move definitions match the displayed types, power, and PP. Shadow Convergence is Dark, physical, power 90, 5 PP. Follow-up owner screenshot https://i.e-z.host/📸/qkuqttm6.png confirms: Deals Physical damage to the target and removes 1 Ultimate Energy from it. Your Evomon gains 1 Ultimate Energy and 2 stacks of [Growth].

Abyssal Flame III: Dark, special, power 230. Deals Special damage to the target. If this skill defeats the target, the next opposing Evomon to enter battle takes 100 fixed damage. Rank III uses the standard ultimate level 150; lower ranks are not supplied. Lightning 8 is energy cost, not PP. No Skill Cache assignment has been supplied.

Initiate: The first 2 skills always act first. Switching does not cancel it. The displayed 1.5% is not part of the effect text.
